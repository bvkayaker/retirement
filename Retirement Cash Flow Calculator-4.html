Federal tax uses 2026 brackets and standard deductions (married filing jointly, or single after a death), indexed to your inflation rate. Social Security is taxed under the provisional-income rules. Sell and move (age 0 = no sale)
%
At the sale the mortgage is paid off, gain above the home-sale exclusion ($250,000 single, $500,000 married) is taxed at the capital gains rate, and the rest goes to the taxable account. If you buy, the new price comes out of the proceeds and counts as home equity. After the move, the old mortgage payment stops and the new housing cost starts. Adjust spending if the move changes it.
Annual gifting to children
$19,000 is the 2026 federal annual exclusion per giver per recipient. Gifts leave your accounts and estate; amounts above the exclusion use lifetime exemption and need a gift tax return. Gifts and the exclusion both inflate at the general rate (approximate). Gifting appreciated assets means your children lose the step-up in basis they would get by inheriting.
Life insurance
If the insured person is still covered when their plan ends, the benefit goes to the taxable account the following year, income-tax-free. Premiums are fixed and stop at death or when coverage ends. Estate tax and policy loans are not modeled.
